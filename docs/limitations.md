# Limitations

- The synthetic suite uses repeated authored phrases and schema shapes. Recorded lineage clustering does not remove all phrase-bank dependence; it is a controlled finite fixture design, not a deployment sample.
- External evidence is dominated by 184 protocol schemas. The 24 runtime artifacts cover two curated benchmark tasks; they are not 24 independent runs or 208 executions. Historical demonstrations were not independently rerun or certified as unedited raw captures.
- External intervals condition on selected coarse groups and fixed source composition. Shared protocol conventions cross groups, and two runtime task clusters cannot support strong generalization. Different source/record/corpus weighting produces different estimates.
- Authentic object sizes, field lengths and byte inflation differ from the pilot. A numerically larger external effect does not identify a causal between-dataset difference or a universal effect size.
- Formatting adds bytes and often absolute tokens. Increased relative t180k advantage does not recommend indentation as a token-minimization policy. The compact corpus tokenizer ranking reverses relative to pretty formats.
- t180k does not always beat BPE: one compact and 16 spaced external counterexamples are retained. Sensitivities are descriptive and do not replace the frozen primary endpoint.
- Identical inference pretokenizers do not make the no-whitespace/t180k comparison a single-variable curriculum intervention. Their vocabularies, merge order and observed colon-space coverage differ. Regional boundary accounting is observational, not causal mediation.
- The intentional colon-space constraint, whitespace merging and punctuation/newline design are already documented by the original SuperBPE work. No first-study, new-bug or priority claim is established.
- The audit-approved upstream backend is not proven fully equivalent to the custom fork. Shared training-selection metadata does not prove identical historical corpus bytes or a fully shared nominal stage-1 prefix.
- No LM training, downstream accuracy, agent reasoning, end-to-end latency or commercial billing result is measured.
- The public tree exposes exact statistical replay from saved counts, not all original raw inputs. Full synthetic encoding replay requires authorized private access because the owner chose to keep the fixtures and generator private. Upstream rights apply to downloaded sources and tokenizers; no blanket repository license can replace them.
